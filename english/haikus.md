# Haiku Collection

A curated set of original haikus on nature, technology, and solitude.

---

### Morning Dew

Dew drips from the leaf
A spider weaves silver thread
Dawn paints the sky gold

---

### The Server Room

Blinking lights hum low
Cables tangled, fans spinning
No one hears them cry

---

### Ocean at Dusk

Waves crash on the shore
The sun melts into the sea
Seagulls call goodnight

---

### Empty Chair

Dust gathers softly
A chair waits by the window
Gone but not forgot

---

### Compiler Error

Semicolon missed
The build fails at line forty
Coffee fuels the fix

---

### Autumn Walk

Leaves crunch underfoot
Wind sweeps through the amber trees
Cold hands find warm pockets

---

### Frost on Glass

Ice blooms on the pane
Winter breathes in crystal signs
Morning waits outside

---

### Old Codebase

Functions without tests
Comments lie, the docs are gone
Logic lives alone

---

### Rain at Midnight

Drops tap on the roof
Thunder rolls through sleeping streets
Puddles catch the moon

---

### First Light

Pale glow through the trees
Birds begin their dawn refrain
World wakes without sound

---

### The Git Merge

Two branches collide
Conflicts bloom in every file
Peace through careful hands

---

### Bamboo Grove

Stalks sway in the breeze
Green shadows dance on the path
Silence fills the air

---

### Last Login

Cursor blinks and waits
No new mail, the screen goes dim
Logout without words
