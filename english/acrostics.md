# Acrostics

## Acrostic #1: UNSAFE

Under the watchful eye of night,
Nothing is quite as it seems,
Silence envelops the weary world,
Awakening shadows from forgotten dreams,
Finding our way through the dark,
Every step forward leaves a mark.

## Acrostic #2: BOUNTY

Brave adventurers seek the prize,
Over mountains, under skies,
Unsolved puzzles call their name,
Never-ending is the game,
Treasures wait for those who try,
Your reward is waiting, high.
