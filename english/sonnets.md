### Sonnet I — The Weight of Stars

Beneath the weight of stars I stand alone,
The night wraps round me like a velvet shroud,
My voice returns as echo, carved in stone,
A whispered prayer dissolving in the cloud.
The hours drag slow, each minute feels a year,
I trace the constellations with my hand,
And find no comfort in the turning sphere,
No solid ground on which I dare to stand.
The morning comes but does not banish dark,
The sun itself seems weary, old, and cold.
I carry on, though something misses mark,
A story that grows tired before it's told.
  Yet still I search the heavens for a sign,
  And call this battered, searching heart still mine.
